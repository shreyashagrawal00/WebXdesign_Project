## 2025-05-15 - Improving Form Interactivity and Accessibility
**Learning:** Adding `isLoading` states to authentication forms significantly improves UX by preventing duplicate submissions and providing immediate feedback. Properly linking labels to inputs via `htmlFor` and `id` is a fundamental accessibility requirement that is often overlooked but easily fixed.
**Action:** Always check for label-input associations and add loading states to buttons that trigger network requests.
